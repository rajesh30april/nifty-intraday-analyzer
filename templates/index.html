🎯 How the app picks strike & lots at entry:

1. Strike: ATM = nearest 50-point level to Nifty spot. Pick ITM3→OTM3 above — ITM = deeper in the money (higher delta, costlier), OTM = out of the money (cheaper, more lots).

2. Real premium: Fetches live LTP of the chosen option from Zerodha at the moment of entry — not a formula guess.

3. Lots: floor(₹capital ÷ (LTP × 65)) — uses every rupee efficiently.

Preview uses Black-Scholes with live India VIX + days-to-expiry. Actual lots use real Zerodha LTP at entry.
