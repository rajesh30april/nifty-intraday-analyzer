🎯 How the app picks strike & lots at entry:

1. Strike: ATM = nearest 50-point level to Nifty. App picks 1-OTM (ATM + 50 for CE, ATM − 50 for PE) — cheaper premium = more lots.

2. Real premium: Fetches live LTP of the chosen option from Zerodha at the moment of entry — not a formula guess.

3. Lots: floor(₹capital ÷ (LTP × 75)) — uses every rupee efficiently.

Preview above uses ~0.22% of Nifty as OTM estimate. Actual lots may differ based on real premium at entry time.
