## 🎯 **WHAT EACH CHANGE DOES:**

### **1. SuperTrend: 1.5×ATR → 2.5×ATR**
**Effect:** Allows entries further from the SuperTrend line.

**Example:**
- ATR = 25 points
- **Old:** Must be within 37 points of ST line
- **New:** Can be 62 points away

**When this helps:**
- Price makes a strong move, pulls back 50-60 points, then resumes
- **Old system:** "Too far, wait longer!"
- **New system:** "Close enough, enter!"

---

### **2. Tight Range: 1.2×ATR → 1.8×ATR**
**Effect:** Accepts wider consolidations as "coils."

**Example:**
- ATR = 25 points
- **Old:** Range must be < 44 points
- **New:** Range can be < 67 points

**When this helps:**
- Crude consolidates in a 50-60 point range (still tight for crude!)
- **Old system:** "Not tight enough, skip!"
- **New system:** "Good coil, watch for breakout!"

---

### **3. EMA Cross: 3 candles → 10 candles**
**Effect:** Catches crossovers that happened 10 candles ago (50 minutes).

**Example:**
- EMA9 crossed above EMA21 at 10:30 AM
- Current time: 11:20 AM (10 candles later)
- **Old system:** "Cross too old, ignore!"
- **New system:** "Recent cross, still valid!"

**When this helps:**
- Trend started 30-50 minutes ago, still has momentum
- You catch the middle/end of the move (better than nothing!)

---

### **4. Chart Pattern: 20 candles → 15 candles**
**Effect:** Start scanning for patterns 25 minutes earlier.

**Example:**
- Session starts at 9:00 AM
- **Old:** Wait until 10:40 AM (20 candles × 5 min)
- **New:** Start at 10:15 AM (15 candles × 5 min)

**When this helps:**
- Early breakouts (10:30-11:00 AM)
- **Old system:** "Pattern not ready!"
- **New system:** "Pattern detected, enter!"
